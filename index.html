Performance methodology

Performance is calculated using actual Prudential BID observations from the fund BID history.

Returns are calculated as:

Return % = ((Current BID − Historical BID) ÷ Historical BID) × 100

Funds without sufficient valid BID observations for the selected comparison period are excluded from the ranking.
