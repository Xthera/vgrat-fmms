                How it's worked out. Regular premiums are annualised (monthly × 12, quarterly × 4, half-yearly × 2) and multiplied by the plan's or rider's multiplier. Single premiums and lump sums count at 10% of the premium before the multiplier. For any other production rule, choose “Other eligible base” and enter the eligible base yourself.
